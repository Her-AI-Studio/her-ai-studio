## A Creative Studio for the Next Generation of Women in AI

**Build AI Better. Build Better AI.**

Her AI Studio is a free, beginner-friendly studio program for girls and people who identify as women in grades 9–12. We're building physical AI — built for girls, by girls. Cyberdecks for all!

> A 501(c)(3) nonprofit registered in Massachusetts, USA · EIN 42-3042277

---

## What You'll Build: Your Cyberdeck

Apply to join one of our in-person cohorts. By the end of six weeks, you'll earn your **builder kit** to construct your own custom cyberdeck — a pocket-sized AI computer built inside a case. You'll wire it up, program it, and take it home at the end of the program. Run it offline, with local models, using data you control.

- 🧠 Arduino-powered brain
- ⌨️ Mini Bluetooth keyboard
- 🤖 Local AI Models
- 🎒 Yours to keep

---

## The Program: Three Stations, Six Weeks

| Station | Level | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | Beginner | Demystify AI, meet women shaping the field, build your first locally-powered AI app |
| **Create** | Intermediate | Build your first AI-powered project bridging hardware and software in image, text, or voice |
| **Engineer** | Advanced | Build your cyberdeck — your own AI-powered device. No prior programming experience needed |
| **Showcase** | Capstone | Present your work at a studio showcase to friends, family, and industry guests |

---

## Why We Built This

✨ **Build real things** — Work with real hardware to design and program your own cyberdeck.

♡ **A sisterhood** — Learn alongside mentors, peers, and role models who look like you.

🚀 **Future-ready skills** — Local AI, prompt engineering, machine learning intuition, and ethical AI thinking that schools don't teach yet.
