# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Middle and high school girls, grades 6–12, who are curious about AI but aren't sure they belong in tech. They've heard "AI" in school or online, feel excited but intimidated, and need reassurance before they'll commit. Parents and educators may also arrive at this page, but the primary design target is the girl herself.

## Product Purpose

Her AI Studio is taking shape as a free, cohort-based program for girls to learn to create with AI (chatbots, art generators, apps) in a community of peers and mentors who look like them. **Right now the site collects interest** via Formspree so we can notify people when dates, details, and applications are ready. Success looks like a curious girl or a supportive adult joining the list without friction.

## Brand Personality

Creative, warm, and bold. The studio is a place where art and technology are equally valued. Not intimidating, not corporate. Voice: direct, warm, first-person address. Three words: expressive, welcoming, electric.

## Anti-references

- Cold developer portfolios (dark blue, monospace everywhere, terminal aesthetic)
- Corporate tech landing pages (hero metric templates, enterprise tone)
- Overly "girly" pink-and-pastel designs that condescend to the audience
- Generic SaaS sites (card grids, stat sections, testimonial carousels)

## Design Principles

1. **Belonging first** — Every design decision should make the girl feel she was expected, not tolerated. Direct address ("your seat," "your name") over generic copy.
2. **Art and tech in equal tension** — Neither the studio's creativity nor its technical ambition should dominate. The palette, type, and layout hold both.
3. **Depth over breadth** — The site is one linear scroll. Going deeper into the page should feel like going deeper into the studio.
4. **Earned confidence** — Show what gets made here before asking for anything. Gallery before the interest form.
5. **Concrete over abstract** — Real names, real project titles, real grades. Not "students" but "Luna, grade 9."

## Accessibility and Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced-motion respected for scroll reveals and decorative motion. All form fields labeled. Check contrast for white text and controls on the violet gradient CTA section and for frosted glass panels over the gradient.
